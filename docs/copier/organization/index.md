## Changelogs

The changelog lets people (including you) know what major changes have happened. We generally use this for "version" changes in software. For academic work, this may seem like overkill, especially if it's not enturely clear what changes lead to a new version.

With a calendar based system we can keep a log of changes every one or two months. What are the new features (new figures? new analysis?) or changed methods from the month earlier.

This helps you keep organized, and helps people you may work with follow along with your work. This is especially helpful if you're working with trainees, or if you anticipate someone following up on your work at a later date.
